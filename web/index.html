The Daily Walk

10,000 steps

Every day. It never gets harder.

  • Your character levels from real activity. Steps, workouts and heart rate, read from Apple Health. Nothing to log by hand.
  • A squad is a daily leaderboard. Up to six people, ranked on the day.

What your squadmates can see

Your daily totals: steps, distance, calories and sleep — and only where you have both agreed to share with each other. They never see your heart rate, your workouts, or when in the day you moved.
