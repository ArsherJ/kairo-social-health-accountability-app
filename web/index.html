You've been invited to a Kairo squad.

Kairo reads the activity your phone already records and turns it into a character that levels up. Your day lands on a leaderboard your squad can see — nobody sees your steps.

The Daily Walk

10,000 steps

Every day. It never gets harder.

  • Your character levels from real activity. Steps, workouts and heart rate, read from Apple Health. Nothing to log by hand.
  • A squad is a daily leaderboard. Up to six people, ranked on the day. Set shared goals over a month or a year.
  • Your health data stays yours. Squadmates see a rank and a rating. They never see your steps, your heart rate or when you moved.

Kairo is in a closed beta

It runs on iPhone and is not on the App Store yet. Ask whoever sent you this link to add you to the TestFlight — you'll get an email from Apple with the invite, and your squad code will still be waiting when you open the app.
