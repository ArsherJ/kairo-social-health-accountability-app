# KAIRO Character Bible — MVP

## Identity
**Working name:** KAIRO Creature
**Species inspiration:** Philippine eagle, stylized into an original cute fantasy mascot.
**Role:** The player's living avatar and emotional center of KAIRO.

> Your real life powers your character.

The creature is eagle-inspired, not a realistic Philippine eagle and not a literal national-symbol illustration.

## Personality
Friendly, curious, warm, energetic, playful, encouraging, never intimidating.

## Visual Style
Minimalist, rounded, soft, cute, expressive, clean silhouette, warm, mobile-first.

Avoid realistic anatomy, aggressive bird-of-prey styling, dark fantasy, excessive detail, military/patriotic styling, or generic fitness-mascot aesthetics.

## Proportions
- Oversized head
- Compact torso
- Large expressive wings
- Small feet
- Distinctive fluffy head feathers
- Large readable eyes
- Small simplified beak

Target visual balance: head 50–60%, body 25–35%, legs/feet 10–15%.

## Attribute Language

### Intelligence — Sleep
Head/face is the primary visual region.
- Sleepy: half-closed eyes, droopy eyelids, messy feathers, lowered head, occasional yawn.
- Normal: neutral expression and posture.
- Well rested: bright eyes, perky feathers, alert posture, subtle sparkle.

Sleep state is daily, not permanent progression.

### Strength — Workouts
Torso/chest/wing structure is the primary region.
- Slim
- Fit
- Strong

Strength changes gradually with progression; it should not flip dramatically every day.

### Agility — Steps / Running
Agility is primarily communicated through motion, not muscular legs.
- Low: slow movement, small steps, wings tucked.
- High: energetic hops, active wing motion, running, gliding/flying, dynamic idle.

## Locked Features
Never casually change:
1. Head silhouette
2. Eye placement
3. Beak shape
4. Head-feather silhouette
5. Wing attachment points
6. Body proportions
7. Foot shape
8. Base palette

## MVP States
Sleepy, Normal, Well Rested.

Useful reactions: Happy, Excited, Tired, Victory, Level Up.

## MVP Poses
Idle, Sleep, Walk, Run, Workout, Race Victory.

## Cosmetics
MVP target: 12. Use modular slots:
- Head
- Face
- Body
- Back
- Neck
- Feet
- Effect

Do not alter canonical anatomy unless explicitly making a full-body skin.

## Philippine Identity
Keep it subtle: eagle-inspired silhouette, local/nature/island themes, occasional Filipino-inspired cosmetics. Avoid forcing flags or national colors into the base character.

## Golden Rule
> One recognizable creature. Many states. Many outfits. Same identity.
