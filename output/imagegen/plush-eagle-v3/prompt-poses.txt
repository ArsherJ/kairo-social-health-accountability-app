KAIRO plush-eagle v3 — the four Motion poses
============================================

Run each pose as an identity-preserving EDIT of the approved v3 base render, one call per
pose, with `--input-fidelity high`. The API default is `low` and drifts one bird into four
different birds across a set — this is not optional.

Compose each call as:

    IDENTITY CLAUSE
    + the pose paragraph
    + the base prompt's Rendering / Palette / Technical background sections, verbatim

State the pose POSITIVELY, as its own instruction. An edit told only "make it run" redraws
the bird standing still; that is what the first stage-art run shipped.


IDENTITY CLAUSE (prepend to every pose)
---------------------------------------
Redraw the bird from the input image in a new pose. Keep exactly the same bird: the same
proportions, the same head and body shapes and their taper, the same crest tuft count, shape
and colour, the same eye size, spacing and colour, the same beak, the same blush, the same
wing shape, the same two ochre legs with three spread toes each, the same outline weight, and
the same flat palette. Change ONLY the pose described below. Do not age, fatten, slim, or
restyle the bird, and do not add or remove any feature.


POSE — idle          (Motion: branch, under 25% of the day's steps)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
The bird stands square and upright facing the viewer, both feet flat on the ground side by
side at the same height, taking its weight evenly, head level, with both wings held down and
folded in against its sides.


POSE — walk          (Motion: treeline / valley, 25-75%)
--------------------------------------------------------
The bird is mid-stride, walking towards the viewer: one leg is lifted clear of the ground and
swung forward so that its foot sits well above the other, and the other leg is planted and
carries the weight. The body leans very slightly forward. Both wings are held a little away
from the body for balance. The lifted foot is drawn open, with the same three spread toes as
the planted one, never as a closed fist or a stump.


POSE — run           (Motion: climb, 75-100%)
----------------------------------------------
The bird is mid-run: the body leans forward, both wings are raised and swept out and back away
from the body, one leg is lifted high and forward with the foot well clear of the ground, and
the other is planted beneath the body. The crest tufts stream slightly backwards with the
motion. The lifted foot is drawn open, with the same three spread toes as the planted one,
never as a closed fist or a stump.


POSE — ridge         (Motion: the 10,000-step finish line, cleared)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The bird stands tall and triumphant, facing the viewer, both feet planted flat on the ground
side by side at the same height and set slightly wider apart than in the input image. Its
chest is pushed forward and its head is tilted a little upward. Both wings are raised and
spread open and upward into a wide V, held clear and away from the body on each side, with
both wingtips fully visible. Its crest tufts stand up perked. Its beak is open in a broad
happy smile. Nothing rises above the crest tips.


Notes for whoever runs these
----------------------------
- `ridge` is new. Today the code draws the same `run` art at 75% and at 100%, and the
  finish-line arrival is a momentary celebration owned by `daily_walk`. This pose is the
  persistent look for the rest of a cleared day, which is a deliberate change to that rule.
- Framing is not the model's job. `normalise()` in `scripts/generate_stage_art.py` trims each
  render to its own alpha and re-lays it against the base's bounding box — same canvas, same
  centre line, same feet-on-the-bottom ground line, same figure height.
- Nothing may rise above the crest tips in any pose. `scripts/generate_crest_masks.py` finds
  the crest as the topmost opaque row inside the central 44% of the canvas; a raised wingtip
  in that band gets tinted instead of the crest. This is why `ridge` spreads its wings wide
  and outward rather than straight up over the head.
- After any new render lands, rerun `scripts/generate_crest_masks.py`. Every render needs a
  mask beside it and `character-assets.test.ts` fails if one is missing.
