# KAIRO Character, Solo & Squad Gameplay Design

## 1. Core Direction

KAIRO should take inspiration from the simplicity of Stompers without copying its exact mechanics.

The core principle is:

> **Real life → Character → World**

Instead of feeling like a fitness tracker that simply displays numbers, KAIRO should make the player feel that their real-world activity is powering and developing their character.

The goal is:

> **“Look what my real-world activity did to my character.”**

KAIRO should feel like a combination of:

- Simple social fitness competition
- Cute character attachment
- Light RPG progression
- Real-world activity tracking
- Casual adventure/game mechanics

The design should stay friendly, warm, approachable, and easy to understand rather than becoming a complicated hardcore RPG.

---

# 2. Character Philosophy

KAIRO can have multiple species, but species should **not permanently represent a specific attribute**.

For example:

| Species | Visual Identity | Possible Natural Tendency |
|---|---|---|
| Wolf | Athletic, energetic | Agility |
| Bull | Big, sturdy | Strength |
| Deer | Lightweight, graceful | Endurance |
| Bear | Chunky, powerful | Strength/Vitality |
| Cat | Cute, agile | Agility |
| Turtle | Small, sturdy | Endurance |
| Fox | Clever, energetic | Balanced |

However:

> **Species = Identity**

> **Stats = Behavior**

> **Clothing = Customization**

> **Equipment = Progression**

> **Activity = Power**

The player should not feel locked into a particular build because of the species they chose.

A Wolf can eventually become extremely strong.

A Bear can become extremely agile.

A Deer can become a powerful all-around character.

This keeps species fun and collectible without making the initial character choice permanently determine gameplay.

---

# 3. Character Customization

KAIRO does not need dozens of completely different character models.

A better approach is:

> **One excellent base character design per species + lots of customization.**

This is similar to the appeal of minimalist avatar systems: the underlying body stays recognizable while players personalize the character.

# 4. Character Visual Progression

The character can subtly reflect the player's development.

The visual changes should not necessarily be dramatic.

Examples:

### High Strength

- Slightly stronger silhouette
- More powerful idle animation
- Strength-oriented poses

### High Agility

- More energetic movement
- Faster idle animations
- Lightning/spark effects

### High Endurance

- Confident running/exploration animations
- More resilient visual personality

### High Overall Activity

- Glow
- Aura
- Special effects
- Higher-level cosmetic details

The character becomes a visual representation of the player's lifestyle.

---

# 5. Simplifying the Attributes

The initial attribute system can be reduced to three core attributes:

## ⚔️ Strength

Primarily generated through resistance/strength activities.

Examples:

- Gym workouts
- Calisthenics
- Push-ups
- Squats
- Weight training
- Resistance exercises

Strength should eventually power combat/battle mechanics.

---

## 🏃 Endurance

Primarily generated through sustained activity.

Examples:

- Running
- Walking
- Cycling
- Swimming
- Longer exercise sessions

Endurance should eventually power long-distance journeys and expeditions.

---

## ⚡ Agility

Primarily generated through movement frequency and steps.

Examples:

- Daily steps
- Walking
- Short movement sessions
- General mobility

Agility is ideal for fast movement and race-style challenges.

---

# 6. What About Vitality?

Vitality can eventually be removed as a permanent attribute.

Instead, it could become a temporary condition/energy system later.

For example:

> ❤️ Vitality: 82

It could rise with consistent activity and decrease after prolonged inactivity.

This makes Vitality more useful as a temporary gameplay state instead of becoming another permanent stat that users have to understand.

For the MVP:

> **Strength + Endurance + Agility is enough.**

---

# 7. The Core Gameplay Philosophy

KAIRO should not make users think:

> “I need to calculate Strength, Agility, Endurance, and Vitality before I can understand this challenge.”

The complexity should happen underneath the system.

The player should simply see:

> **RUN TO THE SUMMIT**

> 3.2 km

> YOU 72%

> FRIEND 68%

> **KEEP GOING!**

The game handles the underlying calculations.

---

# 8. Three Core Challenge Types

KAIRO can eventually have three major challenge types:

## 🏁 Race

Primarily:

- Agility
- Endurance

Concept:

> **Who gets to the finish line first?**

This is the closest to the simplicity of Stompers.

Example:

```text
🏁 WEEKEND DASH

🐺 YOU       ━━━━━━━━━━━━●━━ 🏁
🦊 MARK      ━━━━━━━━━●━━━━━ 🏁
🐻 ANNE      ━━━━━━━●━━━━━━━ 🏁
🐰 JOHN      ━━━━━●━━━━━━━━━ 🏁

8,421 steps
```

The important visual is not the number.

The important visual is:

> **The characters are racing.**

The numbers can remain secondary.

---

# 9. Battle

Primarily:

- Strength

Concept:

> **Defeat a boss.**

Example:

```text
             🗿
        ANCIENT GOLEM

        ███████████░
             HP 64%

 🐺 YOU              🦊 MARK
 82 ATK              71 ATK

    💥                  💥
```

Players contribute Strength toward damaging the boss.

Example:

- You: 82 damage
- Mark: 71 damage
- Anne: 64 damage
- John: 58 damage

Unlike Race, Battle can be cooperative.

The group is not fighting each other.

They are fighting the boss.

---

# 10. Adventure

Primarily:

- Endurance
- Distance
- Potentially mixed attributes later

Concept:

> **Travel somewhere together.**

Example:

## 🗺️ Expedition: Mount Kairo

```text
🏔️
│
│       🐺
│      🦊
│
│   🐻
│
│ 🐰
│
└────────────────
   Base Camp
```

Everyone's walking/running contributes to the party's progress.

Example:

- You — 14.2 km
- Mark — 11.7 km
- Anne — 9.4 km
- John — 6.2 km

Party progress:

> **41.5 / 50 km**

Adventure is ideal for groups that want to work together rather than compete.

---

# 11. Squad Members

The same group of friends can participate in all three challenge types.

Example:

## KAIRO Squad

- 🐺 You
- 🦊 Mark
- 🐻 Anne
- 🐰 John
- 🐱 Mike

The squad can create:

### 🏁 Race

> “First to 20,000 steps.”

### ⚔️ Battle

> “Defeat the 10,000 HP boss together.”

### 🗺️ Adventure

> “Travel 100 km together this week.”

The participants remain the same.

The gameplay changes.

---

# 12. Different Participant Models

The challenge types do not need to support exactly the same number of people.

## Race

Possible:

- 1v1
- Small group
- 10+ participants

Everyone competes against everyone.

## Battle

Possible:

- 2–10 players

Everyone works together against a boss.

## Adventure

Possible:

- 2–20+ players

Everyone contributes to the journey.

This creates different reasons to invite friends.

---

# 13. Challenge Creation

The challenge creation flow should stay extremely simple.

## Step 1 — Create Challenge

Tap:

> **+ Create Challenge**

## Step 2 — Choose Type

### 🏁 Race

> Compete against your friends.

### ⚔️ Battle

> Work together to defeat a boss.

### 🗺️ Adventure

> Travel the world together.

## Step 3 — Choose Friends

Example:

```text
☑ Mark
☑ Anne
☑ John
☐ Mike
```

## Step 4 — Define Objective

Examples:

- First to 10,000 steps
- Defeat 10,000 HP boss
- Travel 50 km together
- Complete challenge before Sunday

The system should handle the complexity.

---

# 14. Challenge Lobby / Details Screen

Each challenge can have a simple lobby/details page.

Example:

## 🏁 Weekend Sprint

---

# 15. Three Screens or One Challenge Area?

Instead of making Race, Battle, and Adventure feel like three completely separate systems, use one main:

# 🏆 Challenges

With three modes/tabs:

> **[ Race ] [ Battle ] [ Adventure ]**

Each tab displays active and completed challenges.

This keeps navigation simple.

The user understands:

> Challenges = social gameplay.

The three challenge types are simply different ways to play.

---

# 16. Solo Mode

KAIRO should keep the existing:

> **Solo Mode**

But Solo should not simply be another version of Squad Challenges.

The clean mental model is:

> **Solo = My journey**

> **Squad = Our challenge**

Solo exists so that a player can enjoy KAIRO even without friends.

A user should be able to download KAIRO, choose a character, start moving, and progress without needing anyone else.

---

# 17. Solo Gameplay

Solo is primarily about:

- Personal progression
- Character leveling
- Exploration
- Quests
- Unlockables
- Achievements
- Character customization
- Personal goals

The player asks:

> **“What am I doing with my character today?”**

---

# 18. Solo World

Give the player a small personal world/map.

Example:

```text
🗺️ KAIRO WORLD

🏠 Village
   │
   ├── 🌲 Whispering Forest
   │
   ├── 🏔️ Iron Mountain
   │
   ├── 🌊 Azure Coast
   │
   └── 🔥 Kairo Temple
```

Real-world activity helps the character progress through this world.

Examples:

### Walking

> +2 km

Character travels 2 km.

### Strength workout

> +Strength XP

Character gains the ability to overcome physical obstacles.

### Step goal

> Unlock treasure chest.

This turns fitness progress into an adventure.

---

# 19. Solo Quests

Solo is a natural home for daily and weekly quests.

Example:

## 🌅 Today's Quests

### Walk 5,000 steps

`███████░░░ 72%`

Reward:

- ⭐ 150 XP
- 🪙 30 Kairo Coins

### Complete a workout

`○`

Reward:

- ⚔️ +100 Strength XP

### Move for 30 minutes

`████░░░░░░`

Reward:

- 🏃 +100 Endurance XP

The loop becomes:

> **Real activity → Complete quest → Earn XP → Level character → Unlock things**

---

# 20. Solo Should Not Become Another Challenge Mode

Initially, avoid creating:

- Solo Race
- Solo Battle
- Solo Adventure

That can create unnecessary complexity.

Instead:

## Solo

> **Your personal journey.**

## Squad

> **Challenges with people.**

The three major challenge mechanics primarily belong to Squad.

---

# 21. Relationship Between Solo and Squad

This is the most important structural relationship.

Your Solo progression should affect your Squad performance.

For example:

```text
             SOLO
               ↓
       Build your character
               ↓
       Strength / Endurance /
            Agility
               ↓
             SQUAD
               ↓
      Use your character
      in social challenges
```

The player improves themselves in Solo.

Then they bring that progress into Squad.

# 23. The Core Loop

The overall KAIRO loop becomes:

```text
REAL-LIFE ACTIVITY
       ↓
   Earn XP / Stats
       ↓
   DEVELOP CHARACTER
       ↓
  SOLO PROGRESSION
       ↓
   LEVEL / UNLOCK
       ↓
   JOIN SQUAD
       ↓
  SOCIAL CHALLENGE
       ↓
Race / Battle / Adventure
       ↓
  Rewards / XP / Items
       ↓
  DEVELOP CHARACTER
       ↓
        LOOP
```

This creates a natural relationship between fitness and game progression.

---

# 24. MVP Recommendation

Do not build the entire RPG immediately.

The first version should be very simple.

## Phase 1 — Race

Start with:

> **Steps → Character Movement → Finish Line → Winner → XP → Cosmetic Reward**

This is your Stompers-inspired core loop.

The user should immediately understand it.

---

## Phase 2 — Strength

Add:

> **Workout → Strength XP → Character Development → Battle**

Introduce bosses and cooperative combat.

---

## Phase 3 — Endurance

Add:

> **Walking/Running → Endurance → Long-Distance Adventure**

Introduce map-based group expeditions.

---

## Phase 4 — Advanced Systems

Later you can introduce:

- Mixed-stat challenges
- PvP
- Seasonal events
- Boss events
- Clans
- Guilds
- Quests
- Equipment
- Power-ups
- Special abilities
- More species
- More worlds
- Events
- Leaderboards

These should be built on top of the core system rather than being required from the beginning.

---

# 25. Design Philosophy

KAIRO should follow:

> **Simple to understand, deep underneath.**

The player should never need to understand the entire game system before playing.

They should understand:

> **“I move in real life, my character gets stronger, and I can use my character to do cool things with my friends.”**

The complexity can exist behind the scenes.

---

# 26. The Core Product Statement

The strongest way to think about KAIRO is:

> **KAIRO turns your real-world activity into character progression and social gameplay.**

Or even more simply:

> **Your real life powers your character.**

That should guide the character design, animation, challenge system, progression system, and UI.

---

# 27. Final Character Concept

The KAIRO character is not just an avatar.

It is:

- A representation of the player
- A visual record of their progress
- A participant in their personal journey
- A competitor in races
- A fighter in battles
- An explorer in adventures
- A collectible/customizable identity

The ultimate feeling should be:

> **“That's my KAIRO.”**

And the product loop should ultimately feel like:

> **Solo makes me stronger. Squad gives me a reason to use it.**
