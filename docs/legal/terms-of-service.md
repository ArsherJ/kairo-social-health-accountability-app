## 2. Kairo is not a medical or fitness advisory service

**This is the most important term here, so it is not buried in clause 14.**

Kairo turns activity data into a game score. It is entertainment and social
accountability. It is **not** medical advice, not a training programme, and not
a diagnostic tool. It does not know your medical history, your injuries, your
medications or your limits.

- Consult a doctor before starting or changing an exercise routine.
- Do not chase a score, a streak, or a squadmate at the cost of your health.
- The scoring ceiling is deliberately reachable by ordinary daily activity. It
  is not a target you must hit and there is no penalty for a zero day beyond
  losing a streak.
- Stop if you are in pain. A broken streak is a game mechanic; an injury is not.

Data from Apple Health is an estimate produced by your device. Active energy in
particular is estimated, and a phone in your pocket measures a gym session
poorly. Treat every number in Kairo as approximate.

## 5. Scores, goals and squads

**Nobody else can reduce your score.** Your daily score is computed from your own
health data and nothing else. Kairo previously included a sabotage mechanic that
let squadmates take points off your day; it was removed in August 2026 and no
equivalent exists.

A **goal** is a target you set for yourself, or one your squad commits to
together, over a window of days you choose. Goals affect **your standing in Kairo
only** — they change nothing about your health data, which is never modified, and
nothing outside the app.

Once created, a goal's target and dates are fixed: changing them would re-grade
days already counted. You can abandon a goal at any time. On a shared goal, the
members are fixed when it is created, so somebody joining or leaving the squad
later does not change what the group committed to.

Completing a goal awards XP and a permanent record on your profile. It awards no
money and no thing of monetary value.

## 6. Fair play

Do not fake activity data — by shaking your phone, by strapping it to an animal
or a machine, or by writing synthetic data into Apple Health.

We run automatic plausibility checks and may flag a day whose data is not
physically consistent. A flagged day may be excluded from leaderboards.
Persistent fabrication may cost you your account.

Kairo is provided **as is** and **as available**. Specifically, we do not promise

- it will be available without interruption;

We may change, suspend or discontinue any part of Kairo. During the beta this is
likely rather than theoretical.
